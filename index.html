Our story

จากครัวของยายFrom Grandma's kitchen, not a factory

Yaiya began at a worn wooden table in the South, where our grandmother — ยายย่า — pounded chilli pastes by hand and folded miang kham for anyone who walked in. Nothing was measured; everything was tasted.

We still pound, never blend. We still roast over real heat. Every jar is filled in small batches so the flavour stays as loud and bright as it was at her table.

How we make it

ทำมือทุกขั้นตอนSlow, by hand, in small batches

1 · Source

Betel leaves, palm sugar and chillies from Thai growers we know by name.

2 · Pound

Stone mortar, never a blender — so the oils release slow and the paste keeps its grain.

3 · Roast

Real heat coaxes the smoke and caramel out of every chilli and shallot.

4 · Jar

Filled the same day, sealed by hand. No preservatives, nothing to hide.
